# Privacy Policy Tabata Timer: Simple HIIT

**Last updated:** 2026-03-27

SimpleTabata ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

## Information We Collect

### Data you provide
- **Workout settings and preferences** (such as timer durations, phase toggles, sound volume, and color customizations) that you configure in the app.

### Automatically collected information
- **Purchase and subscription status**: Apple provides purchase/entitlement information via StoreKit to determine whether Pro features are available. We do not receive your payment details.
- **Device/app technical information**: Apple and the operating system may provide limited technical signals needed for in-app purchase flows and app functionality.

## How We Use Information
We use the information to:
- Provide core timer functionality and save your settings.
- Enable or disable Pro features based on your StoreKit entitlements.
- Restore purchases and resolve purchase states.
- Improve stability and troubleshoot issues (based on runtime behavior, not personal data).

## Data Storage
- Your settings are stored **locally on your device** using iOS storage mechanisms (e.g., UserDefaults / local files).
- We do **not** operate our own servers for collecting or storing your workout content.

## Sharing of Information
We do not sell your personal data.

The app may share information only in these cases:
- **With Apple**: for processing purchases, subscriptions, and providing entitlement information via StoreKit.
- **When required by law**: if we must comply with a legal obligation.

## Third‑Party Services
The app uses:
- **Apple StoreKit** for in-app purchases and subscription/lifetime entitlement verification.

Apple’s handling of information is governed by Apple’s policies.

## Children’s Privacy
SimpleTabata is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your Choices
- You can reset the app’s locally stored settings using the **Clear data** action inside the app (if available).
- You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
